USHJA International Hunter Derby & National Hunter Derby Horses for Sale

Hunter derby horses require a rare combination of natural hunter movement, genuine scope for high option fences, and the athleticism and adjustability to excel across varied terrain in the handy round. The USHJA International Hunter Derby has become the most prestigious and popular hunter competition in the sport, and demand for quality derby horses has grown significantly at every level.

USHJA International Hunter Derby Horses: The pinnacle of hunter sport, the USHJA International Hunter Derby features courses set at 3'6" to 4'0" with high option fences at 4' and above. The ideal International Derby horse has natural scope, a flowing hunter jump style, and the mental and physical ability to gallop and rate across a varied course of natural-style obstacles. International Derby horses must be USHJA enrolled and have demonstrated consistent performance at the highest level of hunter competition. The prestigious Platinum Performance/USHJA International Hunter Derby Championship is held annually at the Kentucky Horse Park.

USHJA National Hunter Derby Horses: The National Hunter Derby was developed as a stepping stone to the International Derby, with courses set at 3' with high option fences at 3'5". National Derby horses are ideal for junior and amateur riders looking to develop a talented young horse or compete at the derby level without the height demands of the International program. The National Derby offers Open, Amateur, and Junior sections, making it one of the most accessible and exciting derby programs in hunter sport.

Amateur Owner Derby Horses: Amateur owner riders compete in dedicated derby sections at both the International and National level. An amateur owner derby horse must combine the scope for high option fences with a safe, rideable temperament for non-professional riders — one of the most coveted horse types on the hunter market.

Hunter Ponies for Sale — Small, Medium & Large

Hunter ponies are among the most beloved and competitive animals in the sport. Elite Equine Marketplace lists hunter ponies across all three sizes from trusted sellers worldwide:

Small Hunter Ponies (12.2hh & under): Compete at 2'3" and are ridden by junior riders age 12 & under. Small ponies are typically a child's first show pony — safe, well-trained, and competitive at local and rated shows through Pony Finals.

Medium Hunter Ponies (13.2hh & under): Compete at 2'6" and are ridden by junior riders age 14 & under. Medium ponies are one of the most sought-after pony types — the perfect size for a growing junior rider stepping up from smalls.

Large Hunter Ponies (14.2hh & under): Compete at 2'9" and are ridden by junior riders age 17 & under. Large ponies are the most competitive pony division, with the best large ponies rivaling horses in quality of movement and jump style. Qualified for Pony Finals large ponies are among the most valuable ponies on the market.

Green Hunter Ponies: Young or inexperienced ponies with the movement and temperament to develop into competitive show ponies. Green ponies are ideal for programs looking to develop a pony for the division or for families seeking a long-term partner for a junior rider.

Short Stirrup Horses and Ponies: Safe, confidence-building horses and ponies for the short stirrup division — one of the most popular introductory divisions at local and schooling shows. Short stirrup horses are ideal for young children just beginning their show career.
